A trustworthy chain of custody for your evidence

Record every evidence file, control who may hold it, document each transfer, and confirm at any time that nothing has been altered. Files stay on your computer and are never uploaded.

How it works

  1. 1
    Authorize custodians

    List the people and organizations allowed to hold the evidence.

  2. 2
    Log evidence

    Add each file with a category. The app records its unique digital fingerprint.

  3. 3
    Transfer custody

    Release items only to authorized custodians, with the purpose recorded.

  4. 4
    Verify anytime

    Instantly detect any altered file or tampered record.
